## Current decision

Keep Microsoft on watch and do not initiate from this analysis. Microsoft's
operating franchise and cloud/AI growth are strong, but the intrinsic methods
do not establish adequate valuation support at the saved market observations.
The principal decision variable is whether current infrastructure investment
is a temporary buildout that will produce durable incremental cash flow or a
persistent capital requirement.

The integrated pro-forma-driven FCFF workbench produces a base value of
**$238.17 per diluted share** and causal downside/base/upside values of
**$187.75/$238.17/$290.97**. Its FCFE cross-check is **$279.60**. Both remain
below the saved September 28 market close of **$509.22**. The earlier
starting-FCFF DCF produced **$114.68**, while peer P/E produced
**$470.89-$551.77** and the qualified Oracle EV/revenue cross-check produced
**$360.90** around a September 10 close of **$492.44**. These methods are not
averaged because they treat reinvestment, cash flow, capital structure, and
accounting earnings differently.

## Evidence stack

### Operating evidence

FY2026 revenue was $331.839 billion, operating income was $155.237 billion,
operating cash flow was $182.935 billion, cash PP&E additions were $115.948
billion, and finance-lease asset additions were $24.608 billion. The gap
between operating growth and reinvestment is why accounting earnings and cash
flow produce different valuation signals.

### Integrated valuation

The workbench projects FY2027-FY2031 statements and computes FCFF from NOPAT,
depreciation, stock compensation, cash capex, finance-lease additions, and
changes in operating working capital. At a 10.202614% WACC and 3% terminal
growth, it produces:

- Enterprise value: **$1.809 trillion**
- Equity value after adding $73.043 billion of non-operating cash and deducting
  $106.888 billion of borrowing and finance leases: **$1.775 trillion**
- Value per FY2026 weighted-average diluted share: **$238.17**
- Terminal value as a share of enterprise value: **80.3%**

### Causal scenarios

| Case | Revenue-growth shift | Gross-margin shift | Annual cash-capex shift | Value/share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Downside | -2.0 pp | -0.5 pp | +$20.0bn | $187.75 |
| Base | 0 | 0 | 0 | $238.17 |
| Upside | +2.0 pp | +0.5 pp | -$20.0bn | $290.97 |

The scenarios are causal combinations, not assigned probabilities. Even the
upside case remains below the September 28 market reference.

### Locked cash-capex sensitivity

The one-at-a-time locked test reduced FY2030 cash capital spending from $155
billion to $125 billion and FY2031 spending from $160 billion to $120 billion,
with every other model input held constant. Intrinsic value increased from
$238.17 to $285.08 per share, matching the pre-run directional prediction. The
result moved the valuation closer to initiation but remained below the saved
$509.22 market reference, so the watch/defer recommendation did not change.

This result is conditional rather than a complete operating forecast. It
assumes Microsoft can reduce capital spending without weakening revenue growth
or margins. If lower infrastructure investment constrains Azure or AI capacity,
the valuation benefit would be partly or fully offset. The complete prediction,
timestamp, preserved output, and reconciliation appear in the validation and
AI-use record.

## Conditions and monitoring triggers

The committee should reconsider the watch/defer call if subsequent evidence
shows that operating cash flow is growing sustainably faster than combined
cash PP&E and finance-lease additions, with improving utilization and margins.
The valuation should then be rerun using contemporaneous WACC inputs, price,
diluted shares, and the latest filing. Deteriorating cash conversion, persistent
high replacement spending, or weaker AI monetization would reinforce the
decision not to initiate.

## What changed from Edition A to Edition B, and why?

The recommendation did not materially change, but its basis did. Edition A
identified cash conversion after reinvestment as the decisive unknown. Edition
B tested that question through a linked five-year pro forma, multiple valuation
methods, causal cases, the locked cash-capex sensitivity, and validation
checks. Those results support remaining on watch because the intrinsic cases
do not reach the saved market prices and because the model remains highly
sensitive to terminal assumptions and the treatment of infrastructure
investment.
