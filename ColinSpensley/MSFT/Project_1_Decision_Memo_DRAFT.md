# Microsoft: Watch/Defer Pending Evidence of AI-Infrastructure Cash Returns

**To:** Buy-side Investment Committee  
**From:** Colin Spensley, Analyst  
**Decision:** Do not initiate at the saved market observations; retain MSFT on watch  
**Company evidence cutoff:** July 29, 2026  
**Market references:** September 10 and September 28, 2026, as labeled

## Recommendation and committee ask

Keep Microsoft on watch rather than initiating a position. Approve quarterly
monitoring of cash conversion from AI and cloud infrastructure and require a
valuation rerun when new evidence supports a materially different FCFF path or
when the market price provides a defensible margin relative to the updated
range.

forecasts future operating cash conversion.

## Investment case

Microsoft reported FY2026 revenue of $331.839 billion and operating income of
$155.237 billion. Azure grew 43% in Q4 and Microsoft 365 Commercial cloud grew
14%. The company has scale, distribution, recurring enterprise relationships,
and a credible path to monetize cloud and AI demand.

The counterweight is capital intensity. FY2026 operating cash flow was $182.935
billion, cash PP&E additions were $115.948 billion, and finance-lease asset
additions were $24.608 billion. The investment question is therefore not simply
whether demand grows; it is whether incremental capacity earns returns above
its cost and produces cash available to investors.

## Material assumptions and sensitivities

The integrated base assumes revenue growth fading from 15% to 10%, gross margin
recovering from 67.8% to 68.5%, cash capex rising from $135 billion to $160
billion, WACC of 10.202614%, and 3% terminal growth. The causal cases change
growth, gross margin, and cash capex together. A separate WACC-growth grid spans
**$186.19-$329.96 per share**. Terminal value supplies **80.3%** of base
enterprise value, so long-run inputs remain load-bearing.

In the locked one-at-a-time test, FY2030 cash capex decreased from $155 billion
to $125 billion and FY2031 cash capex decreased from $160 billion to $120
billion. All other assumptions remained constant. Intrinsic value increased
from $238.17 to $285.08 per share, but remained below the saved $509.22 market
reference. The result supports continued monitoring rather than initiation and
depends on Microsoft reducing investment without impairing growth or margins.

## Risks and failure conditions

- The model may understate normalized cash flow if the current infrastructure
  cycle is temporary and utilization rises rapidly.
- It may overstate value if AI capacity requires persistent replacement spending
  or competitive pricing prevents adequate incremental margins.
- Maintenance and growth capex are not disclosed separately.
- Constant diluted shares do not capture forward stock-compensation dilution.
- Market price and WACC inputs are not from one contemporaneous timestamp.
- Flat residual balance-sheet buckets simplify the forecast.
- Peer comparisons are affected by business mix and investment gains.

## Monitoring and reversal triggers

Reconsider initiation if quarterly operating cash flow grows sustainably faster
than cash PP&E spending plus finance-lease additions, supported by improving
utilization, margins, and disclosure on incremental returns. Refresh WACC,
price, diluted shares, and the equity bridge at the same timestamp. Deteriorating
cash conversion, persistent high replacement spending, or weaker monetization
would reinforce the decision not to initiate.
